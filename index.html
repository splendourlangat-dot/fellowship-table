Who We Are

About Fellowship Table

A fellowship devoted to the discipleship of the nations through the teaching of God's Word.

Our Purpose

Fellowship Table exists to help believers grow in their understanding of Jesus Christ, the finished work of the cross, and their identity in Him.

Through biblical teaching and fellowship, we desire to see believers established in the truth, strengthened in faith, and equipped to live out the life of Christ.

We welcome everyone who desires to learn, grow, and deepen their relationship with the Lord.

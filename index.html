Who We Are

About Fellowship Table

Fellowship Table exists for the discipleship of the nations through the teaching of God's Word and the revelation of the grace of Jesus Christ.

We gather to learn, grow in faith, ask questions, and encourage one another as we continue in the truth of Scripture.

Our desire is to see believers established in Christ, transformed by the Word, and equipped to share the Gospel with others.
